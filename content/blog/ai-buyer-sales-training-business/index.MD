I build AI roleplay buyers for sales training. It's a realtime voice AI your reps practice against, out loud, that evades and resists the way a real prospect does and only opens up once it's been approached well. Every call ends with a scored breakdown.

Objection-handling practice where the first fumble lands on a machine instead of on live pipeline.

## Your reps learn on pipeline you paid to generate

A junior rep hits the hard objection for the first time on a real call. He freezes, over-explains, and talks the buyer out of the deal. That was pipeline your marketing spent real money to create, gone on one rep's first fumble.

The usual fixes barely move it. Role-play with a colleague caves the second the pressure feels real, call recordings only show you the damage once it's done, and no manager can sit in on every run. So the practice that actually matters keeps happening on your best leads.

## What you get

- **A private dojo for hard conversations.** Reps run around 40 practice calls before they ever speak to a live prospect.
- **A buyer that doesn't fold.** It evades, blocks, and only opens up to a patient, indirect approach, so a good score has to be earned. Objection-handling scores climb from roughly 2.4 to 4.1 out of 5 in a rep's first month.
- **Faster reps.** Ramp time to a first closed deal drops about a third.
- **Coaching without the calendar.** Every call ends with a scored debrief in the rep's own words, quoted back as evidence, so managers coach without booking an hour per rep.

## Why it holds

It's real voice, both ways. The AI hears tone and hesitation, and it waits through a silence instead of trampling it, because tone is what sales calls get won and lost on. The buyer runs on per-scenario personas you control, so it behaves the way your market actually behaves. And it's access-safe: no credential ever reaches the rep's browser, which is what lets you roll it out to a whole sales floor.

One honest boundary: the score measures conversation craft against a rubric. It doesn't measure revenue, and it won't replace the senior who works a real room. It replaces the part of learning that used to cost live leads.

## Who this is for

Sales enablement AI for teams that onboard reps at volume, or that sell against hard objections where one bad first call gets expensive. The shape carries well past selling: support and customer success, recruiting, procurement negotiation, and the manager training for feedback and salary talks everyone keeps postponing. Anywhere a conversation is expensive to get wrong.
